## What it does

Catches a thought that is not an action, without making you decide where it
belongs.

## Procedure

1. Write the thought as the **Note** title, in the user's own words where
   possible. Do not tidy it into something duller.
2. Set **Type**: Idea, Question, Reference, Doubt or Connection. Pick the
   nearest and move on — it is rough sorting, not a taxonomy.
3. Add a **Summary** of a line or two: what it means and why it mattered
   just then. The page body takes the longer version.
4. Set **Project** if it is obvious. Leave it empty if the thought has not
   found a home yet — that is a legitimate state, and one of the reasons
   this database exists.
5. Set **Source** if it came from somewhere.
6. Set **Status** to Open. Every note starts there.
7. Confirm in one line.

## Rules

- Never convert a note into a to-do on your own initiative. The split
  between the two lists is the thing that keeps both usable.
- **Status only moves when the thought has actually been dealt with** —
  Filed once it has found its home, Actioned once it became a to-do or a
  decision. A list where everything is Open forever is exactly what this
  is meant to prevent, and it is also why you must not quietly mark things
  Filed to tidy up.
- Preserve the user's phrasing. A half-formed thought recorded in its
  half-formed words is more useful later than a polished paraphrase.
